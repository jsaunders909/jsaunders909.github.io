Hi! I'm Jack, a Senior Research Scientist at [Tavus](https://tavus.io/), where I work on next-generation avatar rendering and generation technology. I completed my PhD at the University of Bath, supervised by [Vinay Namboodiri](https://vinaypn.github.io/) and [Darren Cosker](https://www.cs.bath.ac.uk/~dpc/), as part of [ART-AI](https://cdt-art-ai.ac.uk/) and [CAMERA](https://www.camera.ac.uk/). I also founded [RealSyncAI](http://realsyncai.com/), providing consultancy for Avatar companies, and have previously interned at Microsoft and Epic Games.

My research focuses on deep learning for Digital Humans — in particular, expressive and identity-preserving facial animation in both 2D and 3D. Currently I'm most interested in **video diffusion models** as a path to photorealistic, real-time avatar generation, including techniques like Self-Forcing and other autoregressive methods that eliminate exposure bias and push towards real-time deployment. I also work on Gaussian Splatting, Flow Matching, GANs, and audio-visual multimodal AI.
